SEBI risk profiling requires information including age, investment objectives and time horizon, income, existing investments or assets, risk appetite or tolerance, and liabilities or borrowings.

The investment adviser should assess the risk a client is willing and able to take, including capacity for absorbing loss and whether the client is unwilling or unable to accept risk of loss of capital.

Suitability requires investments to be appropriate to the client's risk profile and supported by a documented process that considers investment objectives and financial situation.
